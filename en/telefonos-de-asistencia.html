    Roadside assistance numbers

    If your car breaks down or you've had an accident, the first step is to call your insurer's assistance line. Here are the car insurance numbers of the main insurers.

    After the tow truck, call us

    Ask for the car to be taken to Green Car Service Tenerife in Las Chafiras. We'll handle the rest with your insurer.

    922 73 64 47WhatsApp 674 06 13 71

    Before you call

    Have the registration, your policy number and the exact location (kilometre marker or address) ready. The assistance number also appears on your policy and receipt.

    If anyone is hurt

    Call 112 first. Signal the vehicle, put on your vest and get off the road on the safe side.
