    Eco-balance eco-friendly paint

    Caring for the environment has been one of our commitments since we started in 2019. That's why our paint shop uses «eco-balance» eco-friendly paint.

    Why we chose an eco-friendly paint

    Benefits for the environment

    «Eco-balance» paint brings direct environmental benefits compared with more polluting options.

    In line with how we work

    We aim to offer a complete, sustainable service, and paint is a big part of what we do every day.

    Without compromising quality

    Finish and durability matter just as much: our paintwork comes with a lifetime paint warranty.

    What's included

    Eco-friendly paint isn't a separate extra: it's simply how we paint in our workshop.

    Solid and metallic colours

    We apply «eco-balance» paint in solid and metallic colours, matching your car's shade.

    Applied in the booth

    We paint in a booth, a controlled environment that helps achieve a good finish and a cleaner process.

    Full preparation

    Surface preparation is the same as in any quality job: sanding, primer and clear coat.

    Lifetime paint warranty

    The work is backed by our lifetime paint warranty.

    If your car has a special finish, such as some pearlescent colours, we'll confirm in the estimate how we'll handle it and with which products.

    What painting more sustainably means

    Painting a car involves chemicals, solvents and waste. Much of a paint shop's environmental impact comes from volatile organic compounds, the substances that evaporate while paint is applied and dries. That's why the industry has been moving towards lower-solvent products, such as water-based paint, and more controlled processes.

    At Green Car we've chosen «eco-balance» eco-friendly paint, which has direct benefits for the environment. It fits with one of our three pillars, commitment, which includes respect for our surroundings and sustainable practice. You can read more on our sustainability page.

    If you'd like to know more about the product itself or how we handle waste from the paint shop, just ask. We'd rather explain it clearly than use the word eco-friendly as a mere sales line. Being open about how we work is part of earning your trust.

    Will I notice a difference in the result?

    What you'll notice is the finish you'd expect: a well-matched colour, a good shine and a protective layer that stands up to the sun. Sustainability doesn't come at the expense of quality. Surface preparation, painting in the paint booth and clear-coating are done with the same care, and the work comes with a lifetime paint warranty.

    • For body and paint repairs after a knock.

    Sustainability beyond the paint

    Better painting is only part of it. Repairing a panel instead of replacing it, when that can be done properly, also cuts waste and material use. And protecting the metal well against corrosion extends the car's life, which is probably the most sustainable thing of all: keeping it in good shape for many years. On an island, where everything has to be shipped in, making a car last longer counts even more.

    If your insurer is paying for the repair, we use the same paint: you can choose your repairer and bring the car to Green Car. See how it works on our insurer liaison page. For anything else, ask for an estimate and we'll explain how we'll go about it.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does eco-friendly paint last less?

    Not necessarily. Durability depends mostly on good preparation and the clear coat, and our paintwork comes with a lifetime warranty.

    Does it cost extra?

    It's the paint we work with, not an add-on. The price of each job depends on the repair and we set it out in the estimate.

    Does it work for every colour?

    We use it for solid and metallic colours. For special finishes, we confirm in the estimate how we'll handle them.

    Can I have it if my insurer is paying?

    Yes. If you bring the car to our workshop, we paint it the same way, whoever pays for the repair.

    Common misconceptions

    What people often believe, and what actually happens

    Six ideas we often hear at the garage that are worth clearing up.

    “If I don't go to the main dealer, I lose my warranty.”

    European rules let you service your car at an independent garage without losing the manufacturer's warranty, as long as its specifications are followed and suitable parts are used.

    “My insurer decides where my car is repaired.”

    As a rule you can choose the garage. Some policies offer perks at their partner garages: we help you read yours before you decide.

    “A small dent can wait.”

    A scrape that lifts the paint leaves bare metal exposed. With the sun and salty air in the south of the island, corrosion sets in sooner than you think.

    “Eco-friendly paint is lower quality.”

    A paint with fewer solvents can give an equally durable finish. What makes the difference is the preparation and the application.

    “An electric car doesn't need a garage.”

    It has fewer wearing parts, but brakes, tyres, suspension, air conditioning and battery cooling still need checking.

    “You only need to check the car before the MOT.”

    The ITV checks minimum standards at a given moment. Regular maintenance is what prevents costly breakdowns and nasty surprises on the road.
